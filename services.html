01 / 04

Founder programming

Stanford Founders runs a regular speaker series and workshops for members. Sessions cover fundraising, recruiting, go-to-market, and other operational topics, led by founders and partners.

What it is
A standing programme of talks and hands-on sessions, led by the founders and partners who have done the work themselves.
Who it's for
Members at any stage who want operational depth on fundraising, recruiting, and go-to-market.
What to expect
Speaker series and firesides with operators. Fundraising, recruiting, and go-to-market workshops. Office hours with partners and angels.
02 / 04

Co-founder matching

We host curated dinners and introductions across the graduate schools to help members find co-founders. Members are paired by background, sector, and stage.

What it is
A matching programme that puts members from different graduate schools at the same table, on purpose.
Who it's for
Members looking for a co-founder. Pairings are made by background, sector, and stage.
What to expect
Curated co-founder dinners. Profile-based introductions across schools. Office hours for matched pairs.
03 / 04

Sector mixers

Sector-focused gatherings for members working in similar areas, so the people in the room share your problems, not just your campus.

What it is
Smaller gatherings organised by track rather than by school or class year.
Who it's for
Members building in a specific field who want to meet the others working in it.
What to expect
Recent tracks: Bio & therapeutics · Climate & energy · Defense & deeptech · AI infrastructure · Fintech & payments
04 / 04

Investor access & Demo Day

Stanford Founders supports member fundraising through partner introductions and the annual Demo Day, held each spring. The next Demo Day is in May 2027.

What it is
Warm introductions when you are ready to raise, and a stage in front of investors once a year.
Who it's for
Members who are raising or preparing to. Demo Day is open to all members.
What to expect
Partner introductions at active venture firms. Pre-pitch reviews with alumni founders. Demo Day each spring.
Sector tracks

Five tracks, five rooms.

Bio & therapeutics Climate & energy Defense & deeptech AI infrastructure Fintech & payments

Mixers are organised by track, so members working on similar problems end up in the same room. Recent tracks include bio and therapeutics, climate and energy, defense, AI infrastructure, and fintech.
